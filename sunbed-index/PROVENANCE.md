# Provenance: Marbella Wire Sunbed Index

- Canonical page: https://marbellawire.com/prices/sunbeds/
- Rows: 8 priced venues + 4 listed as unpublished
- Season: 2026
- Last data update: 2026-08-11
- Last per-venue verification: 2026-08-11
- Method: Prices read from each venue's own website, published rate card, or the booking platform the venue itself sells through, which is labelled “via” wherever the price appears; see each venue's source_url and verified date in sunbed.json. Booking affiliates and aggregators are never used, and a venue that publishes no rate anywhere of its own is listed as unpublished, never estimated.

Exported from the same data files that render the page above; the export
script (in the site's source repo) copies figures only. Editorial text,
photographs and Google Places content (ratings, review counts, coordinates)
are deliberately not included: the CC BY 4.0 grant covers Marbella Wire's
figures, not our prose, not venue photos, and not Google's data.

These two files are byte-for-byte copies of the live endpoints
https://marbellawire.com/data/sunbed-index.json and https://marbellawire.com/data/sunbed-index.csv,
fetched at export time, not a re-computation.
