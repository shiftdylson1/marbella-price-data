# Provenance: Marbella Wire chiringuito census

- Canonical page: https://marbellawire.com/prices/chiringuitos/
- Rows: 43 published venues (of a 100-venue census) + 5 recorded closures
- Season: 2026
- Last data update: 2026-07-25
- Last per-venue verification: 2026-08-17
- Method: Google listing checked per venue; kept only operating venues with their own website publishing a menu

Exported from the same data files that render the page above; the export
script (in the site's source repo) copies figures only. Editorial text,
photographs and Google Places content (ratings, review counts, coordinates)
are deliberately not included: the CC BY 4.0 grant covers Marbella Wire's
figures, not our prose, not venue photos, and not Google's data.

All euro figures are read from each venue's own published menu
(menu_url on every row); price_basis says what a figure is based on.
